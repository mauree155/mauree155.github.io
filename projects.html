Road Speed and Injury Severity in Wales

SAS · Hypothesis Testing · Public Health

A group project analysing 2025 STATS19 police collision data to test whether collisions on faster roads in Wales are more likely to kill or seriously injure someone. We used Pearson correlation across councils, a paired t test within councils and a Mann-Whitney U test on casualty age, each with assumption and robustness checks.

Key finding: 46% of collisions on 60 mph roads killed or seriously injured someone, against 35% across all roads. Serious harm concentrates on fast, rural, single carriageway roads.

Air Pollution and Respiratory Disease in Lagos

Python · Time Series Forecasting · Machine Learning

An analysis of air pollution and hospital respiratory cases in Lagos, Nigeria, from 2021 to 2023. I built a pollution index, compared seasonal patterns across city zones, and tested Linear Regression and Random Forest against Prophet and ARIMA forecasting models.

Key finding: Regression models predicted poorly (R² about 0.04), while the time series models captured yearly seasonality and gave reliable short term forecasts of respiratory cases.

FraudWatch Africa: Mobile Money Fraud Detection

Python · Isolation Forest · FastAPI · Streamlit

An unsupervised fraud detection system for mobile money transactions. I trained an Isolation Forest model on 10,000 transactions, visualised the results with t-SNE and UMAP, and deployed it with a FastAPI endpoint for real time predictions and a Streamlit dashboard for monitoring.

Key finding: 77% of the flagged anomalies came from agent transactions, pointing to where fraud checks would have the most impact.
