Road speed and injury severity in Wales

SAS, hypothesis testing, public health

A group project asking where serious road harm concentrates in Wales after the move to 20 mph limits. We tested the link between road speed and severity with a Pearson correlation across councils (r = 0.59, p = 0.004), a paired t test within councils and a Mann-Whitney U test on casualty age, checking assumptions and robustness each time.

The harm that remains sits mostly on fast, rural, single carriageway roads.

Air pollution and respiratory disease in Lagos

Python, Prophet, ARIMA, random forest

I linked satellite air pollution readings with hospital respiratory cases in Lagos from 2021 to 2023, built a combined pollution index and compared seasonal patterns across the city. Pollutant levels alone predicted cases poorly, so I moved to time series models, which tracked the yearly pattern and gave reliable short term forecasts.

Reporting the weak models honestly was part of the result: it showed where the signal really was.
